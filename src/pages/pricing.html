Pricing questions

What happens after the 14-day trial?

You pick a plan or you don't. We don't take a card up front, so nothing is charged automatically. Your setup and everything {{name}} learned stay for 30 days in case you come back.

Is there a contract?

No. Month to month, cancel from your account in one click. Yearly plans are a discount, not a lock-in: cancel and you're refunded for whole unused months.

What counts as a text?

One outbound message segment to one customer. Replies from customers are free. Review requests by text count; by email they don't.

Do I need to do the morning session every day?

No. Items wait. If you skip a few days, {{name}} trims the list to what still matters and drops what's stale, like a post about last weekend.

What if I have two businesses, not two locations?

Two separate accounts, each on its own plan, or talk to us about Multi-location if they share a brand.

Can I pay with a card, or do you invoice?

Card by default. Yearly plans can be invoiced.
