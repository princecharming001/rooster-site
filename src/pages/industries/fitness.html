Playbooks

Playbooks for the studio

Retention is the business. Acquisition is Instagram and word of mouth. Rooster covers both.

At-risk nudges

Attendance drops trigger a friendly, personal check-in, not a generic 'we miss you.'

Third-class review ask

New members are most likely to review right after they've fallen for the place. Rooster asks then.

Instagram cadence

Class photos, member wins with permission, coach intros. Steady, not spammy.

Trial follow-up

Free-trial visitors who didn't sign up get a short note in the window when most people decide.

Google profile

Class types, schedule, 'beginner friendly,' new photos, the details that make someone walk in.

Challenge and event promos

A seasonal challenge or a charity WOD, promoted to the right members and the neighborhood.
