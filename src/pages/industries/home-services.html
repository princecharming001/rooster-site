Playbooks

Built around how trades actually grow

After-job review ask

Every completed job triggers a request by email or consented text, worded for your trade, timed for the next morning when people say yes.

Quote follow-up

Estimates that sit for two days get a short, friendly nudge. Estimates that sit for two weeks get a different one. You approve each.

Service-due reminders

Tune-ups, filter changes, spring startups and fall shutdowns, based on when you did the last one.

Emergency-ready profile

Your Google profile says 'emergency service,' 'licensed,' and your real service area, because those are the searches that call.

Job-photo posts

Before-and-afters from your camera roll, captioned with the job and the neighborhood. One a week is plenty.

Referral partners

Questions from contractors

I use Jobber or Housecall Pro. Does Rooster replace it?

No. Rooster sits on top. Your job software runs the work; Rooster runs the follow-up, reviews and posts, and reads job history from it where the integration exists.

My customers are older and don't do Instagram.

Then Rooster puts the effort into Google, reviews and reminders, and posts less. You choose the channels.

Can my office manager approve instead of me?

Yes. Add them as an approver and the morning feed goes to them, with you copied.
