Real photos from your camera roll. Captions that name the actual job, the actual street, the actual season.

How it works

From camera roll to feed

Pick the photos, or don't

Connect your phone's job photos or upload a batch. Rooster captions and files them. Or skip photos and let it use your best past ones.

Drafts land in your morning feed

Each one says which photo, which platform, and why this week. You approve, edit, or swap the photo.

Posts go out on schedule

Approved posts publish at the times that have worked for businesses like yours. Results come back into your feed a few days later.

Guardrails

No robot voice

Generic captions are free everywhere now. The point is that yours don't sound generic.

  • Every caption references something specific: the job, the customer's first name if they agreed, the neighborhood
  • Prices and hours in any post come from your verified facts, never guessed
  • Your edits are the training data: change 'delicious' to 'fire' twice and Rooster learns
  • Nothing posts without approval until you turn on auto-post for a type

Questions

Do I need a business Instagram account?

Yes, for publishing through Instagram's official tools. Rooster walks you through converting a personal account in about two minutes. Until then it drafts posts you can copy and post by hand.

Can Rooster make images?

It prefers your real photos and Google's own guidelines ask for photos that represent reality. For text-heavy promos it can generate a simple graphic, clearly labeled.

What about TikTok or Nextdoor?

Not yet. Rooster drafts short video ideas and scripts you can shoot on your phone, and we're working on direct posting.
