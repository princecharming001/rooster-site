How it works

Keeping the listing honest

Questions

Do I have to give Rooster access to my Google account?

Yes, through Google's official sign-in, with only the permissions needed to manage your Business Profile. You can revoke it any time from your Google account.

Will Rooster change my hours without asking?

No. Any change to hours, categories or contact details is a question in your feed first.

What if my profile isn't verified yet?

Rooster walks you through Google's verification during setup. Until it's done, Rooster works with your public listing and drafts everything for you to post by hand.
