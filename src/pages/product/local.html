How it works

From noise to a short list

Rooster scans weekly

Event calendars, local news, new business listings and your competitors' public profiles within the radius you set.

It scores what it finds

Against what has worked for your kind of business, your calendar, and what you've said yes and no to before.

You get three, not thirty

Each with the deadline, the cost, a suggested first message, and a one-line reason. Say yes and Rooster drafts the outreach.

Questions

Does Rooster contact these people for me?

It drafts the message. You approve and it sends from your email, or you make the call yourself with the notes in hand.

How wide an area?

You set it. Most neighborhood businesses use one to three miles. Service businesses that drive to customers often use their whole service area.

What if nothing's worth doing this week?

Then the list is empty and Rooster says so. It won't invent an opportunity to fill the space.
