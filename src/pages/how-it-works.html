Night

While you sleep

Every night {{name}} reads what changed and drafts what needs doing. Nothing is sent. Everything is explained.

  • 11:40 PMReads the day's closed jobs, bookings and receipts from the tools you connected.
  • 1:15 AMChecks Google, Instagram and Facebook for new reviews, comments and messages.
  • 2:30 AMCompares your hours, prices and services across Google, your site and your records. Flags mismatches.
  • 3:10 AMPicks who's due for a reminder, whose quote went quiet, who to ask for a review.
  • 4:00 AMDrafts replies, posts, texts and questions. Checks every fact against your records. Discards anything that fails.
  • 6:45 AMRanks the list, caps it at what fits in fifteen minutes, and puts it on your phone.
Morning

Fifteen minutes, one thumb

Each item shows the draft, a one-line reason, and where the facts came from. Approve, edit, or skip. Ask a question if something's off.

  • Approve sends it on schedule. Edit sends your version and teaches {{name}} for next time.
  • Skip means not now. Reject with a reason means never like this.
  • Questions are one tap: "Google says 9, site says 10. Which?"

Day

Then it ships and learns

Approved items go out at the right times. A few days later, {{name}} checks what happened and adjusts.

  • Posts publish when your audience is usually around. Texts stay inside quiet hours.
  • Replies to your texts land in your feed or forward to your phone.
  • Your edits become rules. Change "we" to "I" three times and {{name}} writes in first person from then on.
  • Results come back: which posts got attention, which reminders got a yes, which review requests landed.

Trust

Earned autonomy

{{name}} starts by asking about everything. It only stops asking about a task type when you've approved that type cleanly, repeatedly, and told it to.

Level 0: tells you

Insights and questions. Nothing goes out.

Level 1: drafts

Every reply, post and text needs a tap. This is where everyone starts.

Level 2: schedules

Low-risk items go out unless you cancel. You opt in per type, after twenty clean approvals.

Level 3: runs

Only for types you choose, like five-star replies. A daily summary. One switch to go back.
