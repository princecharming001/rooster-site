Support FAQ

How do I contact {{name}} support?

The form above, or email {{supportEmail}}. Customers on Full and Multi-location get same-business-day replies. Everyone gets a human.

How quickly will you respond?

Same business day for customers. One to two business days for pre-sales questions. Security reports are acknowledged within one business day.

How do I report a bug or a broken connection?

Use the form with "A connection isn't working" or "Report a bug," and tell us which tool and what you saw. A screenshot helps.

I'm new. How do I choose a plan?

Start free on Full and see what shows up in your feed for two weeks. If you only end up approving reviews and Google items, Starter is enough.

How do I report a security vulnerability?

Email {{supportEmail}} with "security" in the subject. We'll acknowledge within one business day and keep you updated. Please don't test against other customers' data.

How do I cancel?

In the app: your business name, then Billing, then Cancel. Takes effect at the end of the billing period. You can export your data first.
